Question editor

Edit your question sets, preview formatting, and save your changes. Your existing question syntax is supported.

Auto-save is active. Changes are saved locally as you edit. Supports LaTeX with $$...$$, images with ![alt text](image URL), typed answers with (t), and multiple-choice sets.
How to use the editor ⌄
Text-to-speech mode
• Click MATIC answering first to enable auto-answer mode
• Then click TTS to enable voice narration
• TTS will read the question and then the answer
• Adjust speech speed and pause duration using the sliders
• Click TTS button again to stop and disable
• Use EN / FIL toggle to pick English or Filipino voice
Typo handling
• Minor typos are automatically accepted!
• Yellow feedback shows the corrected answer
• Click "Continue" or press Enter to proceed
Keyboard shortcuts
• Press A, B, C, or D to instantly select and submit that option
• Press Enter to submit your typed answer
